# 0.5.1 — 2026-09-25

## Added

- **`regulations_search_rules` filters `cfr_title`, `cfr_part`, `docket_id`, and `rin`**, ANDed with the rest. `cfr_part` takes a part or a range (`"140-143"`) and needs `cfr_title` (`title_required_for_part`); `docket_id` matches the docket numbers the Federal Register prints [#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/47).
- **`citation` + `citation_date` on `regulations_search_rules`** resolve a page cite (`"89 FR 49102"`, `2024-06-11`) to the documents printed on that page, volume 59 (1994) on. `citation_incomplete` and `citation_out_of_range` reject a bad pairing before any request; an empty result reports that day's page span [#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/46).
- **`citation`, `startPage`, `endPage`** on `regulations_search_rules` rows and `regulations_get_document`, null where the Federal Register records no pages [#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/46).
- **`regulationsGovDocketId`, `regulationsGovDocumentId`, `commentCount`, `commentUrl`** on `regulations_search_rules` and `regulations_list_open_comments` rows, read from the Federal Register with no key; `regulations_get_document` adds `commentUrl` and the printed `docketIds` [#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/49).
- **`commentPeriodOpen`** on `regulations_search_rules` rows and `regulations_get_document` (true through 11:59 PM Eastern on `commentsCloseOn`, null with no close date), and on `regulations_get_docket` documents, relayed from Regulations.gov's `openForComment` [#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/50).

## Changed

- **`regulations_list_open_comments`** fills `commentCount` without `REGULATIONS_GOV_API_KEY` and drops the "counts unavailable" notice; `keyed` now reports whether `regulations_get_docket` / `regulations_find_comments` can follow up on a row [#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/49).
- **`docketIds`** is described as the Federal Register's printed docket numbers. `regulations_get_docket`, `regulations_find_comments`, and the `find_comments` empty-docket notice point at `regulationsGovDocketId` instead [#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/49).
- **`regulations_get_cfr_section` `bodyText`** names `citation` + `citation_date` as the path from a source-note cite to its rulemaking [#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/46).

## Fixed

- **`commentsCloseOn` and `commentEndDate`** no longer read as "still open for comment": the first is the inclusive Eastern close date, the second the UTC instant the period ends [#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/50).
- **`regulations_list_open_comments` `asOf`** is today's Eastern date, so documents closing today stay listed after 8 PM EDT [#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/50).
- **`cfrReferences`** keeps parts the Federal Register serves as numbers, which most documents before 2020 do; they were dropped [#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/federal-regulations-mcp-server/issues/47).
